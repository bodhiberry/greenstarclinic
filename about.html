Dedicated to Your Health

Our Clinic

Greenstar Clinic & Diagnostic Center Pvt. Ltd. is a premier healthcare institution committed to precision and reliability. Based in Basundhara, Kathmandu, we serve as a vital link for individuals preparing for foreign employment, ensuring they meet health standards with confidence.

Our facility is equipped with high-end diagnostic technology, managed by a team of experienced clinicians. We prioritize patient-centered care, emphasizing transparency, confidentiality, and technical excellence in every report we issue.

Message from the Chairperson

It is my privilege to welcome you to Greenstar Clinic and Diagnostic Center.

For many in Nepal, foreign employment is more than a job—it is a foundation for a better future. We established Greenstar with the weight of that responsibility in mind. Our mission is to provide an ethical, reliable, and world-class medical examination process that our clients and international partners can trust implicitly.

We believe that integrity and modern science must go hand-in-hand. By investing in modern diagnostic tools and a dedicated professional team, we ensure that every individual who walks through our doors receives the highest standard of attention and accurate medical reporting.

Our commitment goes beyond simple compliance; we strive for efficiency and compassion. We are here to support our youth on their journey abroad by making the medical clearance process as smooth and credible as possible.

Shankar Babu Karki Chairperson Greenstar Clinic and Diagnostic Center Pvt. Ltd. Basundhara, Kathmandu, Nepal
