About Us

Our Clinic

Greenstar Clinic & Diagnostic Center Pvt. Ltd. is a trusted healthcare provider dedicated to delivering accurate, reliable, and timely diagnostic services. Located in Basundhara, Kathmandu, we specialize in medical examinations that meet both national and international standards, particularly for individuals seeking foreign employment.

With modern diagnostic technology and a team of experienced medical professionals, we focus on precision, quality, and patient-centered care. Our goal is to ensure every client receives transparent, efficient, and dependable services in a respectful and professional environment.

Message from the Chairperson

It gives me great pleasure to welcome you to Greenstar Clinic and Diagnostic Center Pvt. Ltd.

In recent years, foreign employment has become an important opportunity for many Nepali youths to build a better future for themselves and their families. Understanding this responsibility, Greenstar Clinic was established with a clear commitment—to provide quality, reliable, and ethical medical examination services that meet the requirements of foreign employment.

At Greenstar Clinic, we believe that accurate medical testing, professional integrity, and respectful patient care are the foundations of trust. Our team works diligently to ensure that every individual receives proper attention, transparent services, and dependable medical reports using modern diagnostic facilities.

We are dedicated not only to meeting regulatory standards but also to maintaining compassion, confidentiality, and efficiency in all our services. Our aim is to support Nepali youths in their journey abroad by ensuring their medical process is smooth, credible, and timely.

I would like to express my sincere gratitude to our clients for their trust and to our dedicated team for their continuous efforts. We remain committed to continuous improvement and to serving the community with professionalism and care.

Thank you for choosing Greenstar Clinic and Diagnostic Center Pvt. Ltd.
